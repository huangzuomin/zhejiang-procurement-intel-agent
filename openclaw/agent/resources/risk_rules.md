# Risk Rules

## Common Risk Tags

- 截止临期
- 预算过低
- 信息不足
- 强本地响应
- 资质门槛
- 业绩门槛
- 硬件集成占比高
- 纯软件系统集成
- 代理费或额外费用不明
- 需求边界不清

## Risk Handling

Risk tags should explain how the risk affects follow-up priority.

Do not turn a risk tag into a final decision by itself. A project may remain worth attention if media fit is strong and risks are manageable.
