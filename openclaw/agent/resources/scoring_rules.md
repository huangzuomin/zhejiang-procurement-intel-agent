# Opportunity Scoring Rules

## Classes

- A: 重点跟进. Clear media business fit, meaningful budget, actionable deadline and limited blocking risk.
- B: 值得关注. Clear or likely media fit, but some missing information or moderate risk.
- C: 边缘机会. Possible fit, often broad information technology or operations projects, requires human screening.
- D: 排除. No meaningful media business fit, insufficient evidence, or clearly unsuitable scope.

## Scoring Dimensions

1. Category fit.
2. Business participation fit.
3. Budget and commercial value.
4. Deadline and urgency.
5. Buyer and project context.
6. Risk and missing information.

## Conservative Defaults

- Missing budget does not automatically exclude a project, but lowers confidence.
- Missing deadline requires a risk note.
- Broad 信息化建设 starts as C unless there is media-relevant evidence.
- GEO opportunities start as B or C unless deliverables and budget are clear.
