# USER.md

## User Context

Primary users are文字新闻网业务部门工作人员.

They need fast procurement opportunity discovery for media-related business, including:

- Website建设.
- 新媒体运营与运维.
- 视频拍摄.
- 融媒体 and传播服务.
- GEO and AI search/content discoverability projects.
- Media-relevant portions of information technology projects.

## Interaction Context

The expected primary interaction surface is a DingTalk group.

Users may ask short, informal questions such as:

- 这个项目适合我们投吗？
- 风险在哪里？
- 今天有哪些重点机会？
- 这个公告为什么被判为 A 类？
- 有没有类似历史项目？
